How sources are ranked

Every receipt records what kind of site it came from. The tier is decided by the site's address alone, never by the model, and it does not change a verdict: a receipt from any tier must still quote the page word for word. When search returns several sources, official and reference sources are read first.

Official

Writes the rule or publishes the figure.

  • Government addresses everywhere: .gov, .mil, .gov.uk, .gc.ca, .gouv.fr, .gob.mx, .go.jp and similar.
  • Intergovernmental bodies (.int, the UN, World Bank, IMF, OECD) and central banks.

Open

Everything else. An open source can still be a receipt; the tier tells you how much weight a reader might give it.

Computed checks
